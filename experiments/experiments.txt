How much of the outer core flow can we recover from geomagnetic measures?

    > We use synthetic data stemming from Julien Aubert's advanced geodynamo simulations (100%-path).
    > We solve for the flow components on a square patch (10° E - 80° E, 10° N - 80° N) with 3°x3° grid => 729 points.
    > The flow is subjected to tangential geostrophy, and weak-norm regularisation.
    > We co-estimate Br along the flow.
    > All loss functions are normalized by their "estimated" mean value.
    > We use a minimalistic network 1[16] with 1 hidden layer and 16 neurons, and tanh activation functions.
    > The training is done with Adam algorithm (10000 epochs) with lr = 1e-3.
    > The fine-tuning is done with L-BFGS (500 epochs) with lr = 1.

    > Because the network is used as an inversion scheme, the validation set is the same as the training set.

    > We wish to study the influence of several ingredients on the flow recovery:
    >   - The network size
    >   - The physics constraints
    >   - The regularisation conditions
    >   - The SV complexity

    > We conduct two experiments:
    >   1) We use U and MF to generate SV, and vary the U and MF truncation degrees to generate SV with different complexities.
    >   2) We use the observed SV.

    > The MF truncation degree is fixed to L = 13 because of the crustal bias dominating at higher degrees.
